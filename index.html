Diesel particulate filter (DPF)

Traps soot from the exhaust and burns it off while you drive. Lots of short trips or stop-start driving can stop it burning off, and the filter loads up.

  • DPF warning light
  • Limp mode or reduced power
  • A warning that keeps returning
  • Fuel economy getting worse

What we do: We clean the filter and check it with a scan afterwards.

EGR valve

Sends a small amount of exhaust back into the engine to cut emissions. Soot and carbon can build up and make the valve stick open or closed.

  • Rough idle or stalling
  • Hesitation when you accelerate
  • Black smoke
  • Check engine light with an EGR fault code

What we do: We clean the valve and check it with a scan afterwards.

  • Diesel cars
  • SUVs
  • Utes
  • 4WDs
  • Light commercial

How do I know if my DPF or EGR valve is blocked?

Common signs are a DPF warning light, limp mode, a rough idle, hesitation when you accelerate and black smoke. Other faults can look the same, so the car needs checking to be sure.

Can you remove (delete) my DPF or EGR valve instead?

No. Removing emissions equipment from a road-registered vehicle is illegal in NSW, so we don't do deletes. We clean the unit so your vehicle stays compliant.

Will cleaning fix the problem?

Cleaning fixes a blockage. It won't fix a cracked filter or a fault somewhere else, such as a sensor. If that's the case, we'll tell you what's needed before you spend more.
